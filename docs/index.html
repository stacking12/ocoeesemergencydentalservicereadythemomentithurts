Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to what people ask us most before their first emergency visit.

Do you really offer emergency dental service 24 hours a day?

Yes. Emergency Dentist 24/7 Ocoee is available around the clock for urgent dental issues, including severe toothaches, broken teeth, and infections. Call 656-212-0359 any time and we’ll guide you on next steps.

Are you open on Saturdays and weekends?

Yes, we see emergency patients on Saturdays and weekends. Dental emergencies don’t wait for business hours, and neither do we.

Can I get a same-day root canal or tooth extraction?

In most cases, yes. Same-day root canals and emergency extractions are among our most requested services, and we prioritize urgent cases to get you seen quickly.

What counts as a dental emergency?

Severe or sudden tooth pain, a knocked-out or broken tooth, facial swelling, an abscess, or a lost filling or crown causing pain are all considered dental emergencies worth calling about right away.

Do I need an appointment to be seen?

No formal appointment is required for genuine emergencies. Call 656-212-0359 as soon as pain starts, and our team will arrange to see you as quickly as possible.
